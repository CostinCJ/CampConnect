# CampConnect Organiser Terms

**Version:** 2026-09-30 (stored with each organisation as `organiserTermsVersion`)

> **Draft — have a qualified data-protection lawyer review these terms before launch.** They set
> out the controller/processor split the app is built around (GDPR Art. 28).

These terms apply between **the organisation** that creates a CampConnect organisation (a camp,
school, club or other organiser, "you") and **CampConnect** (operated by Joldeș Costin-Cristian,
see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) for contact details, "we"). The person creating the
organisation accepts them on the organisation's behalf by ticking the confirmation in the app.

## 1. Roles

- **You are the data controller** for the personal data you and your guides put into
  CampConnect about your camps: the children taking part (their camp/team assignment and anonymous
  app identity), points, announcements, schedules, group photos, map content, and emergency alerts
  (including any location attached to them).
- **We are your data processor** for that data. We process it only to provide CampConnect to you,
  as described in these terms and the app's documentation, and on your documented instructions,
  which are the settings and actions you and your guides take in the app.
- We are a separate controller only for your guides' own accounts (email, display name, sign-in)
  and for running the service safely (security, abuse prevention, crash reports).

## 2. Your responsibilities as controller

You confirm that:

1. You have a **lawful basis** for the children's data you enter. CampConnect is designed around
   legitimate interest in running a camp the child is already enrolled in (see the
   [legitimate interest assessment](kid-data-legitimate-interest-assessment.md)). If you rely on
   something else, you are responsible for it.
2. You **inform parents/guardians** that the camp uses CampConnect and what it involves, for
   example by pointing them to the privacy policy. Taking and sharing **group photos** of children
   needs particular care: make sure your enrolment terms cover it.
3. Your guides use CampConnect only for camp purposes and **keep unnecessary personal data out**
   of free-text fields and notifications (e.g. no full names or medical details in emergency
   alerts, since push notifications are not confidential).
4. You handle data-subject requests (access, deletion, objection) from parents about your camps.
   We will help you (see section 4).

## 3. What we do as processor

- **Purpose and duration:** we process your camp data only to run CampConnect for you, for as long
  as your organisation exists. Camp data is deleted automatically 60 days after each camp ends,
  or earlier when you delete the camp.
- **Confidentiality:** only people who need access to operate the service can access your data,
  and they are bound to confidentiality.
- **Security:** data is encrypted in transit; access is limited to your organisation by
  server-enforced rules; children's journals and passports never leave their devices. See the
  privacy policy for details.
- **Sub-processors:** we use Google Cloud / Firebase (hosting, database, storage, functions,
  authentication, push notifications, crash reports), MapTiler (map tiles), and GitHub Pages (the
  public privacy-policy and TV-leaderboard pages). They are listed in the privacy policy. We will
  tell you before adding or replacing a sub-processor that handles your camp data, so you can
  object.
- **Location:** camp data is stored and processed in the EU; limited transfers by global services
  are covered as described in the privacy policy.
- **Breaches:** we will tell you without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data
  breach affecting your data, with the information you need to meet your own obligations.
- **Assistance:** we will help you answer data-subject requests and, where relevant, with data
  protection impact assessments and consultations with a supervisory authority.
- **End of service:** when your organisation's owner deletes their account, we delete the
  organisation and all its camps, locations, photos and logo. We do not keep copies except where
  the law requires it.
- **Audits:** we will make available the information needed to show we meet these obligations
  and allow for reasonable audits.

## 4. Contact

Use the contact in the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) for requests, questions or to report a
concern about your camp's data.

## 5. Changes

If these terms change materially, the version above changes and existing organisations will be
told in the app before the new version applies to them.
